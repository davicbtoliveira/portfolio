ImageForge is a local-first CLI/TUI for image processing. It focuses on practical
terminal workflows: resize by dimensions or scale, optimize JPEG/PNG/WebP output,
enhance brightness and contrast, inspect image metadata, and chain multiple
operations in a single pipeline.
